# ADR-0006: Do Not Convert AI Clarification into Artificial Percentages

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-09-15

## Context

Conversational AI can produce qualitative judgment but does not automatically provide a validated quantitative score comparable to the deterministic questionnaire calculation.

## Decision

AI clarification may produce a leaning, qualitative confidence/evidence, and summary, but must not invent revised mathematical percentages unless a future validated method explicitly defines them.

## Consequences

Result presentation avoids false precision and keeps deterministic scoring distinct from qualitative AI-assisted reflection.
